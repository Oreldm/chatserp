# ChatSERP privacy policy

ChatSERP is a set of instructions (a Claude Code skill) plus two code templates written as Markdown.

- ChatSERP itself collects, stores and sends **no data**. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking.
- When you use it, Claude works on your own machine and in your own accounts, under your own Claude settings. What Claude reads or writes there is covered by your agreement with Anthropic.
- Plugins you build with ChatSERP are your products. You publish their own privacy policies. The skill has you add one before you submit to OpenAI.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Oreldm/chatserp/issues
